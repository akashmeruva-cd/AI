# Sample Market & Math Queries for Lab 2 (The Market Agent)

Query 1 (Single Stock Math):
"How much would it cost to buy 15 shares of Microsoft (MSFT) at the current market price?"

Query 2 (Comparative Analysis & Allocation):
"What is the current stock price of Apple (AAPL) and NVIDIA (NVDA)? If I have a budget of $5,000, how many whole shares of Apple can I purchase?"

Query 3 (Multi-step Arithmetic with Tax / Fees):
"Calculate the total cost of purchasing 10 shares of Tesla (TSLA) and 20 shares of Google (GOOGL), plus a flat trading commission fee of $15.50 and a 2% brokerage tax."

Query 4 (Market Research & Catalyst Search):
"Find the current price of Amazon (AMZN), look up the recent quarterly revenue headlines, and calculate how much a 10% increase in today's price would be."

Query 5 (Portfolio Rebalancing Calculation):
"I currently own 50 shares of MSFT and 30 shares of AAPL. What is the total current value of my combined tech portfolio?"
